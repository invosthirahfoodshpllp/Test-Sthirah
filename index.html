Why Choose Cane Jaggery Powder?

Jaggery powder is a traditional, unrefined natural sweetener crafted directly from fresh boiled sugarcane juice. Unlike modern white sugar which is stripped of all natural nutrients during heavy industrial chemical processing, our jaggery preserves its natural molasses, offering an authentic, warm caramel depth and robust wholesome flavor profile.

We partner with farmers to bring healthy products to you, ensuring Sthirah Jaggery Powder undergoes rigorous batch testing to guarantee zero chemical additives, zero sulphur bleaching, and zero artificial colorings for modern clean-label kitchens.
